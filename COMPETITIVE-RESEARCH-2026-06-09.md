# Notes — Competitive landscape, 2026-06-09

This is a landscape map for Signal Notes. It is not a feature shopping list. The point is to locate where the field has converged, where the field has fragmented, and where the field has quietly refused — so Signal Notes can sharpen the refusals that already define it and steal only the few patterns that genuinely earn capture speed for the 80% audience. Ten apps were chosen for load-bearing reasons: capture-first (Apple Notes, Google Keep, Drafts), knowledge-first (Notion, Obsidian, Roam, Logseq, Bear), integrated (Reflect), and legacy stalwart (Evernote). Drafts is the closest spiritual sibling and gets the most weight below.

## Patterns Signal Notes already nails

- **Send-to-Tasks as the single, deliberate extract.** Of the ten, only Drafts is in the same conversation, and Drafts is the action-engine extreme. Notes' one-way, user-approved Send is the right reading of that pattern for the 80% — not 100 destinations, one. PRODUCT.md §3 promise #3.
- **Refusal-of-taxonomy.** Notion, Evernote, Obsidian, Bear, Logseq all spend the user's first ten minutes on hierarchy. Notes refuses. The stream + search is the access pattern. Keep and Drafts agree; the rest are wrong for this audience.
- **Private by default, no shared surface.** Reflect has E2EE; Apple Notes has it for locked notes only; the rest are workspace-shared or cloud-readable. Notes' raw-body-stays-private boundary (PRODUCT.md §6) is sharper than nine of the ten.
- **Capture field is the first paint.** The 2026-06-09 textarea raise to `rows=6` (notes side) puts Notes in the small set — Drafts, Apple Notes, Google Keep — where capture is the largest visual weight. Notion, Obsidian, Roam, Logseq, Reflect all bury capture behind chrome.

## Patterns worth stealing

- **Drafts' "every open is a new blank."** Notes already opens with cursor in the capture field; harden the rule that returning to `/app` after writing a note resets to a blank capture, not the last-edited stream position. Adapted for Notes' register: the stream stays beneath, but the cursor is always upstairs.
- **Apple Notes' OCR-of-captured-images.** Not as a feature surface — as a search-fidelity tightening. If the user pastes a photo of a whiteboard, the text inside it should be findable. No "AI" framing; the search just works. Adapted for Notes: ship behind the same flat search field, no toggle.
- **Evernote's Web Clipper speed.** The 1.2-second clip-to-saved is the bar. Notes will ship a clipper one day; when it does, it must match Drafts' textarea budget, not Evernote's form. Adapted for Notes: clipper opens, captures, sends. No notebook picker. No tags.
- **Reflect's meeting-spawned note.** A calendar event becomes a note with attendees pre-populated — but only the title and attendees, never auto-detected action items. Adapted for Notes: deferred behind operator gate; the refusal of auto-detection (PRODUCT.md §8) holds.

## Refusals to harden

- **No backlinks, no graph view.** Roam, Obsidian, Logseq, Reflect all carry this — and all four spend the user's second hour curating it. The 80% will not. BRAND.md §2.2 surface-bloat applies.
- **No required title field.** Bear and Apple Notes already use first-line-as-title; Notion, Evernote, Reflect demand one. Hold the line — the title field is one full second of friction the 80% does not have.
- **No collaborative editing in v1.** Notion and Reflect lean on it; it is the wrong job for a capture surface. The shared-work product is Tasks. PRODUCT.md §7.
- **No AI auto-extraction of action items.** Reflect ships this. The brand refuses it by name (PRODUCT.md §8 "Refusal"). Extraction is the user's deliberate act or it isn't extraction — it's a guess the user has to audit. The 80% will not audit; they will lose trust.

---

The field's mediocrity centroid is this: ten products that conflate *capture* with *knowledge*, then spend their roadmaps on the second word and apologise for being slow at the first. Notion sells a wiki and calls it a notes app. Roam sells a graph. Obsidian sells a vault. Even Apple Notes — the closest to honest — is steadily becoming a scrapbook. Only Drafts holds the line on capture as a discipline, and Drafts pays for it by being a way-station with no memory. Signal Notes' wedge is the unclaimed middle: capture in three seconds, find it later, send to Tasks when the user says so — and refuse, by name, every feature that would slow the first second of that sentence. The competitive opportunity is not to ship more features than the field. It is to ship fewer features than the field, with the field's discipline-of-refusal-as-marketing finally turned into a product surface.
