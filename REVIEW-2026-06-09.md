# Notes review · 2026-06-09

One read-only pass. Mandate: the gap between thought and stored thought is the entire product. Every gram of friction in that gap is the failure mode. Capture should be effortless.

The landing-page wordmark now blinks as a caret from the start (S·70). A held thought, awaiting input. The product surface should live up to that. Mostly it does. The five gaps below are where it doesn't.

---

## 1 · The capture textarea is `rows={3}`. The promise is "three-second capture." The surface looks like a comment box.

**Surface.** `src/app/app/Notebook.tsx:955` — `<textarea rows={3}>`.

**Friction.** PRODUCT.md §4 says the capture field is *the largest element by visual weight on first paint*. A three-row textarea is the visual weight of a form. The N·12 demo correction (2026-05-16) explicitly fought for a "giant capture field" and shipped the demo to match — the live product the demo points at is quieter than its own marketing. Cost is not keystrokes; cost is the gestalt read at first paint. The user sees "leave a comment" and writes a sentence. The product wanted them to write a thought.

**The cut.** Raise the field to a true held-thought weight. `rows={6}` minimum on first paint, or — better — `min-height: 22vh` capped at `min-height: 320px`. The textarea grows with content. The visual stage matches the design budget §9 promises.

**Layer.** Layer 1 (decide). This is a single attribute on a single element and the document already locks the intent.

## 2 · The "misfire toast" is a tutorial in a product that refuses tutorials.

**Surface.** `src/app/app/Notebook.tsx:195–204, 1051` — `maybeShowMisfireToast()` fires `"long-press or use ⌘↵ to send to tasks"` the first time a user clicks a row instead of long-pressing.

**Friction.** Two grams. (a) The product just shipped a commit (96812b4, 2026-06-XX) that *removed* the persistent long-press nudge — correct call — and then added back a session-scoped one. Tutorial-as-toast is still tutorial. PRODUCT.md §7 ("Not configurable", "Not AI-marketed", "no tour, no tutorial, no sample-data offer" in §9) reads against this. (b) The toast says `⌘↵` is how you send to Tasks — but `⌘↵` is *not bound anywhere* in `onCaptureKeyDown` or the document-level keydown handler. The toast advertises a keystroke the product does not honour. That is a worse drift than the friction it was patching.

**The cut.** Delete `maybeShowMisfireToast` and the misfire branch in the row `onClick`. Either bind `⌘↵` to "send the open note to Tasks" (and then the toast can stay), or drop both. Choose. Today the product is inconsistent with itself.

**Layer.** Layer 2 (recommend). Either resolution is brand-coherent; the operator picks.

## 3 · The notebook re-mounts a misfire toast surface and a promote toast surface and an undo toast surface and an in-panel receipt and an sr-only announcer. Five overlapping affordances for "we sent it."

**Surface.** `src/app/app/Notebook.tsx:142, 150, 156, 1233, 1463` — `promoteToast`, `openNoteConfirmId`, `srConfirm`, the in-panel `.open-note-promote-confirm`, plus the misfire toast piggybacking on `promoteToast`.

**Friction.** The behaviour pattern of N·12's "stop twitching the page" was: page holds still. The behaviour pattern here is: every confirm fires through a different primitive. The cost is not user-facing seconds; it is the substrate the product is sitting on. Notebook.tsx is 1541 lines. A capture surface that promises §9 silence-by-default carries five confirmation surfaces. Each one was earned by a row-level taste decision (E2, walkover row 13, NVDA reliability). Together they read as accretion.

**The fix.** One persistent SR-live announcer (correct, keep `srConfirm`). One visible confirmation per action — the in-panel receipt for promotes-from-open-note (correct), the undo toast for delete (correct). Retire the bottom `promote-toast` for the success case; it duplicates the in-panel receipt. Keep it only for the error-with-Retry case, where the in-panel receipt does not fire.

**Layer.** Layer 2 (recommend). Cross-Director: coordinate with Product Taste before cutting — the receipt-and-toast double-confirm may be deliberate accessibility belt-and-braces.

## 4 · The marketing hero is 617 lines of imperative DOM choreography for a product whose H1 is "capture clarity."

**Surface.** `src/components/marketing/notes-hero-voice.tsx` (617 lines). `src/components/marketing/note-anatomy.tsx` (577 lines). `src/app/anatomy/page.tsx` (144).

**Friction.** Capture latency is measured in milliseconds *and* in pre-product-cognitive-load. A first-time visitor lands on a hero that takes ENTRY_SETTLE_MS = 5600ms before the second act begins, then types three phrases on a HOLD_FINAL_MS = 10000ms hold. The visitor is being asked to *watch* before they understand what the product is for. Then `/anatomy` runs a second animated decomposition. Two animated marketing surfaces for a product whose §3 promise is "Capture in three seconds." The brand position §2.2 ("Demo-vs-reality gap") is being violated by the marketing of the product whose entire job is to refuse it.

**The cut.** N·12's instinct was right: the demo should *be* the product. The current `/` shows an animated wordmark of the product, not the product. Recommend: replace `NotesHeroVoice` with the actual `Notebook` rendered against a tiny mock corpus, autofocused, fully usable as the hero. Keep the wordmark caret. Cut `/anatomy` from the home CTA (`page.tsx:101`) or retire `/anatomy` entirely. The product is the demo.

**Layer.** Layer 3 (founder-gate) — this is a material change to the marketing surface and intersects Product Strategy. Brief Ethan; do not ship without his sign-off.

## 5 · `/app` ships four extraction primitives in one panel. The PRODUCT.md §3 promise is "Decide what becomes work" — singular.

**Surface.** `src/app/app/Notebook.tsx:1162–1228` — the open-note panel renders:
- a corner icon-only "Send to Tasks" arrow,
- a "Send as-is" button,
- a "Shape & send" button,
- a `Send to Tasks` button on the drafted-extract row,
- (plus the row's hover ghost `→ Tasks`, the touch long-press tray's "Send to Tasks", and ⌘⌫ as a keyboard delete).

**Friction.** Three "Send to Tasks" buttons exist simultaneously on a single open note in the no-extract state: the corner arrow, "Send as-is", and (after Shape & send → Save) the drafted-extract Send. The Caravaggio walkover row 13 commit (e4d4fe4) is what landed the "Send as-is / Shape & send" pair below the body — earning equal weight was the goal. The cost is that the corner arrow above is now the third sibling, not a fallback. Three surfaces for one decision violates §9's locked visual budget and §7's "Not configurable."

**The cut.** Pick one of two coherent shapes:
- **Shape A (recommend).** Keep the inline "Send as-is / Shape & send" pair. Drop the corner arrow. The corner now carries only the "In Tasks" label when relevant.
- **Shape B.** Keep the corner arrow as the canonical action. Drop the inline pair. "Shape & send" lives behind a quieter affordance — a single text link, "Shape the wording first," below the corner.

Either is brand-coherent. Both-and-and is not.

**Layer.** Layer 2 (recommend). Brief Product Taste before shipping — the equal-weight pair was an explicit taste call by Dalí row 13 and the dissent should be on record.

## STOP doing · the misfire toast

The product just removed the persistent "Long-press any note to send to Tasks" nudge (96812b4) and immediately added back a session-scoped variant. The reason the persistent nudge was wrong is the reason the session-scoped one is wrong: Notes is the product that refuses tutorials. PRODUCT.md §9 — "Empty state on a brand-new account: 'Nothing here yet. Start typing.' Period. Not a tour, not a tutorial, not a sample-data offer." The same posture extends to the row. If the long-press affordance is undiscoverable, the answer is to redesign the affordance, not to ship a toast that points at it.

Compounding the call: the toast names a keystroke (`⌘↵`) the product does not implement. Until it does, the toast is wrong on its face.

**Recommend:** delete `MISFIRE_TOAST_KEY`, `maybeShowMisfireToast`, and the misfire branch at `Notebook.tsx:1050–1052`. Net `-15` lines. The product gets quieter; the affordance still works; the document's posture holds.
