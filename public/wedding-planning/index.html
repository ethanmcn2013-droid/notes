Signal Notes · Wedding planning demo

A venue meeting becomes the work.

Most wedding planning fails between meetings. Decisions get made, actions get promised, and a week later half of it has been forgotten. Here is what one venue meeting looks like in Signal Notes — captured once, turned into clear work without anyone having to act like a project manager.

01

The note, captured during the meeting.

Highfield House · Final tour · Tuesday

Walked through Highfield House with Aoife and Conor. Final tour. Conor wanted to see the back lawn for the ceremony, Aoife pushed for the orangery. Niamh (venue coordinator) said both work, but orangery is +€800 for wet-weather backup. Catering allowance includes a 4-course; they want a vegetarian option for 22 guests. Niamh needs the final dietary list by 4 June, and the final guest count by 12 May. They're holding the date but need a 50% deposit (€4,200) by 2 June. Conor wants to see Lambs Hill before deciding. Photography slots booked. DJ shortlist sent.

02

What Signal pulls out of it — you choose what to keep.

Actions

  • Confirm orangery vs back lawn ceremony location by 2 June · with Aoife & Conor
  • Send final dietary list to Niamh by 4 June
  • Send final guest count to Niamh by 12 May
  • Pay 50% deposit (€4,200) to Highfield House by 2 June
  • Visit Lambs Hill before deciding this week

Decisions

  • Photography slots booked.
  • Catering: 4-course with a vegetarian option for 22 guests.

Open questions

  • Is the orangery wet-weather backup worth +€800?
  • How does Lambs Hill compare — pros and cons before 2 June?

Risk

  • The date is being held, not confirmed. The 2 June deposit deadline is hard. Decide on the venue before that.
03

A short follow-up the couple can read in 20 seconds.

Hi Aoife & Conor — after today's Highfield tour:

  • Niamh needs your final guest count by 12 May and dietary list by 4 June.
  • 50% deposit (€4,200) is due by 2 June to hold the date.
  • Decision needed: orangery (with €800 wet-weather option) or back lawn for the ceremony.
  • I'll arrange the Lambs Hill visit this week so we can compare before committing.

Talk Friday.

The point. Signal Notes promotes context into work only when you choose to. Nothing is auto-detected, no follow-up is sent without your approval. Capture is in plain words. The work that comes out is in plain words too.
